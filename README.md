I am an engineering professional focused on **applied machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, NLP, and vision-language models**. My research explores multimodal misinformation detection with **CLIP, ViLT, SigLIP-2**, metadata and different fusion strategies.

- **Current focus:** reproducible multimodal ML, model evaluation, resource-aware experimentation, and trustworthy decision support.

- **Background:** postgraduate AI research and extensive systems engineering, technical training, and leadership experience.
- **Open to:** AI/ML Engineering, Computer Vision, AI Research Engineering, and academic research collaborations.
